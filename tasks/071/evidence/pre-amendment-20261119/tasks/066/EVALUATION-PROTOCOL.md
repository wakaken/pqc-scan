# Preregistered migration-decision comparison protocol

Status: adopted for preregistration by the maintainer on 2026-11-18; freeze
receipt follows final consistency/review corrections before implementation observations. No new decision implementation results or practitioner observations
have been collected. This protocol is an extension of PQS-QAM Q3/Q5/Q6 and the
existing `tests/quality/judgment` comparison/aggregation, not a new quality system.
Task066 can specify/freeze the protocol; task071 must secure actual resources and
freeze independent case bytes before its observations. Missing resources do not
become successful measurements.

## Questions and claim separation

1. Technical correctness: do expected primitive/parameter/role/assurance facts
   follow the pinned primary references and captured evidence?
2. Conformance: does the implementation follow the approved versioned decision
   table for exactly the declared facts, claims and time?
3. Practitioner usefulness: do declared-role users understand the explanation,
   agree with the proposed response under their explicit organizational policy,
   and complete the task with reasonable effort?
4. Does an ordinal matrix or a justified numerical/MCDA approach add value over
   an explicit decision table/tree? Category labels are outputs, not methods.

Do not pool these claims or call AI review independent human expertise. The main
author has inspected implementation and the current cases are source-exposed.
A01-A18 and task056 remain specification/regression cases, not blind holdout data,
operational truth or an independent population sample.

## Frozen method selection

| Candidate | Selected disposition and rationale |
|---|---|
| M1 explicit categorical decision table/tree | Baseline: exactly the proposed P1-P8 policy, separate sufficiency/readiness, explicit reasons and branch-minimum inputs; implement one product evaluator |
| M2 ordinal matrix with explicit precedence | Selected comparison method: use the same evidence/policy objectives but present an ordinal concern-by-business-impact matrix, with separate deadline and incomplete-evidence rules. Evaluate ordering, action consistency and human effort; do not sum invented numbers or build a second product engine |
| Unweighted numeric count of concerns | Reject as a product candidate: correlated components can double-count one concern, missingness can falsely reduce the value, and count lacks an agreed impact scale. Retain the screening rationale; no unobserved numerical inferiority claim |
| Weighted additive score / weighted MCDA | Not justified for execution without independent scale, tradeoff/weight and missingness authority. No author-invented weights or fit to desired outputs. If task071 obtains a defensible elicited candidate before results, amend/freeze a versioned protocol with human approval before observing it; otherwise the bounded evaluation reports supported non-adoption or insufficient evidence, not a fabricated numeric experiment |
| Lexicographic MCDA | Investigate as a decision-structure alternative: precedence can be specified without arithmetic compensation. Its role is sensitivity/ordering analysis of M2; not a claim that merely renaming M1 is a different method |

M2's fixed matrix (row concern, column declared business impact) is:

| Concern | critical | ordinary | unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| known classical weakness in a relevant role | urgent | urgent | urgent |
| known quantum concern | urgent for relevant signing/authentication; otherwise apply protection/deadline rules | plan only when explicit facts rule out urgent branches | undetermined; preserve known concern |
| bounded role has no known concern | monitor when relevant completeness/use facts suffice | monitor when relevant completeness/use facts suffice | undetermined |
| technical identity/role unresolved or advertisement-only use | undetermined | undetermined | undetermined |

P1 exclusion precedes the matrix. Known deadline pressure and protection/historical-
ciphertext conditions preserve urgent before the ordinary column. Stale due
claims require urgent investigation. Readiness is never a multiplier. Unknown is
not the bottom of an ordinal scale; compare only pairs with justified ordered
urgency and retain unresolved ties/incomparability. The baseline and matrix may
agree semantically: measure representation/effort and defensible ordering rather
than inventing distinct outputs to create an experiment. Any divergence from the
common approved policy is reported as conformance failure, not a competing truth.

## Data, roles and resource plan (D6)

Proposed bounded study: 24 vignettes comprising12 paired case families; two
practitioners independently assess both methods (96 method-case-rater judgments).
Counterbalance method order and pair order using a fixed recorded allocation;
separate training cases from measured cases. Pair members change exactly one
relevant factor: missing vs known input, urgent-but-blocked, same algorithm across
systems, claim expiry, inspection partiality or scoped deadline boundary.

These are proposed resource bounds, not a secured sample or a universal validity
threshold. Limit each practitioner's session to90 minutes plus a separate maximum
30-minute disagreement discussion. Record incomplete sessions; do not fill missing
ratings with the author or an AI. Do not contact participants, spend money or send
external messages under task066 authorization.

Roles required before task071 observations:

- Policy authority: the maintainer explicitly adopts the local scenario policy;
  the organization's actual policy owner must authorize any real operational case.
- Two distinct practitioners with migration/security architecture responsibility;
  record relevant experience and conflicts, actual consent and availability.
- Technical adjudicator with cryptographic/protocol expertise, independently
  checking case facts/source mappings before judgments. This can be a practitioner
  only if the dual role and exposure are declared; two independent practitioner
  ratings are still required.
- Main author/evaluator: implements/collects results and records source exposure;
  cannot silently supply missing independent truth or third-party consensus.

No practitioner, independent author, adjudicator, operational-policy owner or
recruitment slot has been secured by this task. Task071 must record concrete
participants/resource approval and freeze their case/truth provenance. If absent,
keep the affected evaluation unfinished. D6 resource acquisition is a downstream
dependency; approving this plan does not authorize external contact.

## Measures and denominators

| Measure | Unit and denominator | Interpretation and missingness |
|---|---|---|
| Technical fact agreement | Correct independently adjudicated facts / eligible facts, stratified by role/input/assurance | Missing truth is unassessed, not correct; not matcher accuracy |
| Policy conformance | Exact asserted tuple/reasons/claim refs / frozen cases with complete expected tuples | Use existing canonical comparator; missing/duplicate/extra IDs fail infrastructure, unknown expected business truth stays unassessed |
| Harmful under-prioritization | Expected-urgent cases with lower or unjustifiably undetermined response / cases with adjudicated urgent truth and sufficient branch inputs | Do not map unknown to zero. Separate caused-by-missing-evidence cases and loss of known concern |
| Action consistency | Adjudicated acceptable response/action set matched / eligible rater-case-method judgments | Preserve acceptable sets and disagreements; no post-hoc single forced answer |
| Explanation traceability | Correct evidence/claim/source links / required links; separately practitioner explanation rating and cited reason | Link correctness is mechanical; human usefulness rating is not technical truth |
| Missing-data robustness | Paired changes retaining a known concern/urgent condition / eligible missing-data pairs | Each removed input must be branch-relevant or intentionally irrelevant; preserve paired outcomes |
| Sensitivity | Expected directional changes and justified invariances / preregistered one-factor pairs | Same-version determinism separate from changed-policy behavior; no arbitrary perturbation noise as new independent cases |
| Defensible ordering | Justified ordered pairs matched / pairs with adjudicated order; ties/incomparable separately | No total ranking for undetermined cases or readiness-based demotion |
| Effort | Completion time, fields requested/entered, corrections, abandonment per method/rater/case | Report raw paired values and median/range, not invented savings; missing data visible |
| Inter-rater disagreement | Different response/action tuples / doubly rated cases, plus verbatim bounded rationale references | Preserve before/after adjudication. Consensus does not rewrite original ratings |
| Reproducibility | Semantically identical repeated evaluations / repeated fixed-input runs | Pin input/context/as_of/model/catalog/policy and implementation; no population confidence claim |

Zero denominators are undefined/null, never0% or100%. Report tuning/holdout and
all important input/purpose/assurance strata separately. No mean composite score
may hide a failed original requirement. No unapproved statistical significance,
quality threshold or calibrated probability is introduced. Correlated synthetic
pairs and two raters cannot establish universal accuracy or utility.

## Adjudication and D7 disposition

Freeze source-grounded technical truth first. Practitioners independently record
policy/action/effort judgments before discussion. The policy authority resolves
policy interpretation with cited reasons; technical disputes go to the technical
adjudicator. Preserve dissent, undecidable cases and changed policy versions.
Do not use a product observation to retroactively choose expected truth.

Reuse existing source-bound judgment observations, canonical comparison and QAM
aggregation. Extend only the necessary method/rater/effort dimensions under task071;
do not replace old corpus labels, frozen expectations, thresholds or measurements.
Hash complete inputs, truth, instrument, results and tools. Retain all failures,
unassessed cells, overhead and selection limits.

Report supported adoption, supported non-adoption or insufficient evidence for
numerical scoring. Examine scale meaning, normalization, correlated/double-counted
criteria, weight authority, missingness, uncertainty, sensitivity, benefit and
input burden. Missing mandatory comparison/practitioner evidence remains unfinished.
A justified decision to withhold a product score can coexist with completed valid
comparison; an unexecuted study cannot. D7 product adoption separately requires
human approval of formula, scale, model/version, uncertainty and compatibility.
No numerical product score is selected or validated by task066.
