# Original-purpose assessment boundary

The maintainer explicitly accepted correcting the extra completion demands and
continuing work without losing task052's original purpose. The original task
acceptance remains unchanged: Q6-05documents evidence/owners/processes; Q6-06
separates observed controls and proposals; Q6-07assesses reporting/scope/roles
and marks unoperated aspects; Q6-08requires a fixed-tool actual measurement;
Q6-09requires an evidence-grounded self-assessment of all applicable items and
separate application/award facts.

Only Q6-08specifies a new fixed-tool measurement. Q6-09does not require attaining
Passing/Silver/Gold, implementing every practice, creating a historical bug
management system, proving every latent software property, or developing a new
source-statement measurement tool. Those are not silently added closure gates.
All145pinned official items still receive an actual evidence-sufficiency review,
with the official wording, owner, documentation, execution and acquisition scope.
An inspected absence of adequate evidence is a self-assessment outcome. It is
not a positive claim about coverage, exploitability, response rates or safety.

Two distinct fields are required:
- Conformance status/actual uncertainty: Met/Partial/Unknown/Unmet/conditional
  Not applicable. No Unknown is promoted to Met merely to close052.
- Assessment work status: whether the criterion was read, its available evidence
  was actually checked, scope/limitations determined, and a supported claim (or
  inability to substantiate a claim) recorded. An unread/missing reference or an
  unresolved interpretation remains incomplete assessment work.

The old blocking requirement to produce fresh80/90%source-statement rates or a
complete new bug-fix denominator was an added implementation demand. Existing
measurements remain in evidence; their limitations remain in the self-assessment.
No numeric threshold, original requirement, dataset or historical result changes.
The statement and regression-ratio conformity claims remain Unknown when not
supported. This does not satisfy product acceptance or award any badge.

This is more than listing Unknowns: every row receives a current evidence review
with an explicit adjudication, source scope and claim ceiling. Missing Scorecard
execution, undefined reporting ownership, an unchecked source, inaccurate current
settings, unsupported positive claims, or invalid required artifacts would still
prevent closure. Those required task facts/checks must be resolved or handled
exactly as the original criterion permits.

An unanswered optional routine-monitoring inquiry is recorded as unconfirmed; it
does not establish either a running or absent operation. The required reporting
owner/channel/scope/MFAfacts have dated evidence. Publication, settings, product
repairs, new task creation, badge application and054completion remain separate.
